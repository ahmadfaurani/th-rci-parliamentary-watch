## Context Summary

- **Event:** Special Dewan Rakyat sitting, 11 August 2026 (5 days out)
- **Format:** Continuous debate (tanpa waktu rehat), ~35–40 MPs registered to speak (Fahmi Fadzil, Aug 5)
- **Intelligence picture:** Only 11 of 222 sitting MPs have made public RCI statements. 3 consecutive collection cycles (Aug 4–6) with ZERO new MP activity. The RCI has faded from the news cycle 5 days before the debate.
- **Key tension:** Zahid Hamidi (DPM, BN) proposed expanding RCI scope to PH 1.0 and PN 1.0 eras (Aug 5) — this cross-era expansion creates a trap for the PM: it dilutes BN accountability but also exposes PH's 2018–2020 governance period to scrutiny.
- **External shock:** Johor and NS state poll wins (BN "blue wave") have shifted coalition dynamics — BN feels emboldened, PH feels weakened, the RCI has taken a backseat to electoral momentum.
- **Enforcement:** 5 MACC arrests, former CFO questioned 6+ hours (RM370M plantation shares, Section 23 MACC Act), PDRM 5 investigation papers. ZERO MP reaction across 6 collection cycles.

## PIR Definitions

### PIR-PM-01: Debate Participant Identification — Who Are the 35–40 Registered MPs and What Are Their Expected Lines of Inquiry?

**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Identify the names, constituencies, parties, and expected debate topics of the 35–40 MPs registered to speak on Aug 11. Only 11 MPs have made public RCI statements — up to 29 debaters are "first-exposure" speakers with no known position. The PM cannot prepare responses to arguments he cannot anticipate.
**EEIs:**
1. Full list of registered debate participants from the Speaker's office or whips
2. Any MP who has filed formal questions or speaking topics for the sitting
3. Party whip speaking assignments — has each party allocated topics to its MPs?
4. Whether the Speaker has published a debate agenda or order of speakers
5. Any first-time RCI speaker who has signalled their intended focus in any public forum
**Why the PM needs this:** Without knowing who will speak and what they will say, the PM cannot prepare ministerial responses, anticipate attack lines, or identify which allies to deploy in counter-debate. The 3-cycle silence makes this the single largest intelligence gap.

---

### PIR-PM-02: Opposition Attack Architecture — What Is PN's Coordinated 7-Point Demand and How Will It Be Deployed on the Debate Floor?

**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Map PN's coordinated debate strategy, led by Dr Mohd Radzi Jidin (Putrajaya, Bersatu), who has issued a formal 7-point demand list: (1) full original RCI report, (2) per-recommendation implementation status, (3) action plan for unimplemented recommendations, (4) misconduct action status, (5) minister explains all issues, (6) widest Q&A, (7) government commits to MP proposals. Determine how PN plans to deploy these 7 points across its registered speakers.
**EEIs:**
1. Whether PN has assigned specific demand points to specific MPs for the debate
2. Whether Hamzah Zainudin (Opposition Leader) will open or close PN's debate block
3. Whether Tuan Ibrahim (PAS Deputy President) will take a constructive or escalation tone
4. Whether Ahmad Fadhli Shaari (PAS) will pursue the "hidden documents" narrative ("What guarantee is there that nothing else is being hidden?")
5. Whether Radzi will name the RM259M Deru Semangat investment specifically on the floor
**Why the PM needs this:** Radzi's 7-point framework is the most structured opposition attack. The PM must have point-by-point responses prepared — particularly on the "full original report" demand (is the declassified version the complete report?) and the "government commits to MP proposals" demand (which could box the government into accepting opposition amendments).

### PIR-PM-03: Cross-Era Expansion Trap — Zahid's Proposal to Extend RCI Scope to PH 1.0 and PN 1.0 Eras

**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Assess the political risk of the Cabinet-approved cross-era expansion proposal (Zahid Hamidi, Aug 5). This proposal extends the RCI's temporal scope beyond the 2014–2020 BN/UMNO period to include PH 1.0 (2018–2020) and PN 1.0 (2020–2021) governance periods. The PM must determine: (a) does this dilute BN accountability by spreading blame across coalitions, (b) does it expose PH ministers who governed during 2018–2020 to scrutiny, (c) how will PN exploit this to argue the RCI is a political tool rather than genuine accountability, (d) will the Aug 10 BN briefing solidify or abandon this position?
**EEIs:**
1. Which Cabinet ministers supported or opposed the cross-era expansion
2. Whether the AGC has assessed the legal feasibility of expanding an RCI's terms of reference post-publication
3. Whether any PH backbenchers (beyond Hassan Karim) are privately uncomfortable with the expansion
4. Whether PN will use the expansion as evidence of political motivation (Hamzah already questioned the 3-year delay as politically motivated)
5. What the Aug 10 BN briefing (5pm, WTCKL) will decide on this position
**Why the PM needs this:** This is the most dangerous political trap. If the PM supports expansion, he weakens BN's accountability for the core 2014–2020 period and exposes his own coalition. If he opposes it, Zahid and BN may rebel. The debate floor will force a public position.

### PIR-PM-04: Government Response Architecture — Who Will Answer for the Government and on What Topics?

**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Define the government's debate response team and topic assignments. The PM has spoken publicly (Jul 29–Aug 1) but has not indicated whether he will personally respond on the floor or delegate. The Religious Affairs Minister (Dr Zulkifli Hasan) is a Senator presenting the RCI report but cannot be questioned as an MP — a structural gap. Key questions: Who will answer on enforcement (MACC/AGC status)? Who will answer on financial cost (Urusharta Jamaah)? Who will answer on TH Act amendments? Who will answer on implementation status (75% claim)?
**EEIs:**
1. Whether the PM will personally speak or delegate to a minister
2. Which Cabinet ministers will be present and available to respond (Finance Minister, AGC, MACC Commissioner)
3. Whether the 75% implementation claim can be substantiated recommendation-by-recommendation on the floor
4. What the TH Act amendment timeline is and whether draft provisions can be referenced
5. Whether the government has prepared a formal response document or position paper for the sitting
**Why the PM needs this:** Without a designated response architecture, the government will appear unprepared when opposition MPs raise specific recommendation statuses or demand enforcement timelines. The PM needs ministerial topic assignments locked before Aug 11.

### PIR-PM-05: BN Vulnerability Map — Which BN/UMNO MPs Face Reputational Exposure and How Will They Position Themselves?

**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Identify BN/UMNO MPs who are politically vulnerable to RCI findings (2014–2020 was BN/UMNO's governance period) and predict their debate positioning. Ismail Sabri (Bera, former PM) has claimed "Umno has nothing to hide." Zahid (Bagan Datuk, DPM) has said "no one immune" but also proposed cross-era expansion to dilute blame. Key question: will BN MPs take a defensive posture (defend the past) or a reformist posture (distance from 2014–2020 governance)?
**EEIs:**
1. Which BN/UMNO MPs held positions relevant to Tabung Haji during 2014–2020
2. Whether the Aug 10 BN briefing will instruct MPs on a unified RCI position
3. Whether any BN MP will break ranks and demand individual accountability for BN-era figures
4. Whether Zahid's "blue wave" momentum (post-state-polls) will make BN MPs more assertive or more cautious
5. Whether the Bahador Shah (current Baling MP, where predecessor Azeez was TH Chairman) will address his constituency's direct stake
**Why the PM needs this:** BN is the PM's coalition partner. If BN MPs are defensive, they drag the government's position. If BN MPs are reformist, they risk internal party backlash. The PM needs to know which BN MPs to deploy as reformist voices (strengthening the government's accountability narrative) and which to keep quiet.

### PIR-PM-06: Enforcement Narrative — What Can the Government Cite on Aug 11 Regarding MACC/AGC/PDRM Action?

**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Compile the current enforcement status that the government can credibly cite during the debate. As of Aug 6: 5 MACC arrests, former CFO questioned 6+ hours (RM370M plantation shares, Section 23 MACC Act), 3 remanded until Aug 9 (RM450K NOP), ex-CEO questioned, PDRM 5 investigation papers. The PM has said "those responsible will face justice without compromise." The PM needs verified, citable enforcement milestones to substantiate this commitment on the floor.
**EEIs:**
1. Total number of arrests, charges filed, and remand orders as of Aug 11
2. Whether the Aug 9 MACC remand expiry will produce new charges or release before the debate
3. Whether the AGC has approved any prosecution decisions or classified any cases NFA
4. Whether any MACC/PDRM investigation papers have been submitted to the AGC for charging decisions
5. Which specific RCI findings the enforcement actions correspond to (traceability from RCI recommendation to arrest)
**Why the PM needs this:** The opposition will demand enforcement timelines. If the PM can cite specific arrests, remands, and active investigations, it demonstrates action. If enforcement is thin or delayed, the opposition will argue the RCI is toothless. The Aug 9 remand expiry is the last pre-debate trigger.

### PIR-PM-07: PH Backbencher Risk — Which PH MPs Might Break Ranks and Demand More Than the Official Government Position?

**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Identify PH backbenchers who may exceed the government's official RCI position and create internal pressure on the PM during the debate. Hassan Karim (Pasir Gudang, PKR) has already made the most specific accountability demand (citing Section 22(3) TH Act, RM4.8B discrepancy, unlawful hibah, CBT, urged MACC/police/BNM). If other PH backbenchers follow his lead, the PM faces internal dissent on the floor.
**EEIs:**
1. Whether Hassan Karim will escalate or moderate his position on the debate floor
2. Whether any other PH backbencher has signalled intent to exceed the official government line
3. Whether the PKR National Congress (Aug 15–16, 4 days post-debate) is influencing backbencher positioning
4. Whether any Amanah MP (party-level statement demanded MACC/police probes via non-MP Sec-Gen) will echo the party position as a sitting MP
5. Whether the PM's "unity won't be shaken" statement (Aug 1) was a preemptive warning to backbenchers
**Why the PM needs this:** Internal PH dissent on the floor would be the most damaging political image — it would signal government disunity and give the opposition ammunition. The PM needs to identify and manage potential backbencher breakaways before Aug 11.

### PIR-PM-08: Implementation Evidence — Can the 75% Implementation Claim Survive Recommendation-by-Recommendation Scrutiny?

**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Verify whether the government's claim that ~75% of the RCI's 25 recommendations are implemented can withstand per-recommendation scrutiny on the debate floor. The claim was sourced to TH Chairman Abdul Rashid Hussain's cabinet briefing (via Fahmi Fadzil, Aug 5). Radzi has formally demanded per-recommendation status. If any opposition MP presents evidence that specific recommendations are unimplemented, the 75% figure collapses.
**EEIs:**
1. A recommendation-by-recommendation status matrix (25 recommendations, each marked: implemented / partially / unimplemented / unverified)
2. Which of the 25 recommendations the government can evidence with verifiable completion proof
3. Which recommendations are genuinely unimplemented or unverifiable
4. Whether any independent body (Auditor General, PAC, BNM) has validated the 75% claim
5. Whether the PM can commit to a public implementation deadline for remaining recommendations
**Why the PM needs this:** The 75% claim is the government's credibility anchor. If it survives scrutiny, the government appears responsive. If it collapses under per-recommendation examination, the opposition gains a major talking point. The PM needs to know which specific recommendations to defend and which to concede.

### PIR-PM-09: Coalition Leverage — How Will GPS, GRS, and WARISAN MPs Position Themselves and Does Their Silence Signal Leverage-Building?

**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Determine whether the complete silence of East Malaysian MPs (GPS 23 MPs, GRS, WARISAN) on the RCI is strategic leverage-building (waiting to extract concessions in exchange for support) or genuine disinterest. East Malaysian coalitions hold significant leverage in the unity government. If they break silence on the debate floor, their positions could either reinforce or undermine the government.
**EEIs:**
1. Whether any GPS/GRS/WARISAN MP has filed questions for the Aug 11 sitting
2. Whether the RCI's findings on Tabung Haji investments affect East Malaysian depositors disproportionately
3. Whether GPS/GRS/WARISAN silence is coordinated with the government or independent
4. Whether any East Malaysian MP will use the debate to extract concessions on unrelated issues
5. Whether the state poll results (BN gains) have shifted GPS/GRS calculations on coalition loyalty
**Why the PM needs this:** East Malaysian MPs are the government's numerical backbone. If they use the RCI debate to signal dissatisfaction or extract leverage, the PM needs to anticipate their positions and prepare concessions. Their silence is not yet a threat, but unanticipated floor positions could surprise the government.

### PIR-PM-10: Narrative Risk Assessment — Which Narratives Could Escalate Beyond the RCI Into Broader Political/Religious/Racial Framing on the Floor?

**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**Requirement:** Identify which narratives being shaped by MPs could escalate beyond RCI-specific accountability into broader political, religious, or racial framing that damages the government. Ahmad Fadhli Shaari (PAS) has framed the RCI delay as "justice delayed is justice denied" and questioned whether the government is "hiding" other reports. Syed Saddiq (MUDA) has calibrated that the RCI "does not show politicians sakau billions" but warned it "must not be used to clear anyone." The risk is that the debate floor becomes a platform for narratives beyond the RCI's scope.
**EEIs:**
1. Whether any MP will connect the RCI to broader "UMNO corruption" or "Islamic institution governance failure" narratives
2. Whether any MP will frame the RCI as evidence of PH government weakness (inability to govern Islamic affairs)
3. Whether the "hidden documents" narrative (Fadhli) will be amplified by other PN speakers
4. Whether any MP will invoke religious authority (ulama, fatwa) to frame the RCI as a test of Islamic governance
5. Whether social media amplification during the debate will create real-time narrative pressure that forces the PM to respond beyond the RCI's scope
**Why the PM needs this:** The debate is about the RCI, but MPs will use it as a platform for broader political positioning. The PM needs to anticipate which narratives might escape the RCI frame and prepare responses that contain the debate within accountability — not let it metastasize into a referendum on Islamic governance or coalition competence.

## Recommended PM Preparation Actions

1. **Lock the response team** (PIR-PM-04) — Assign ministerial topic areas before Aug 10. Confirm whether the PM speaks personally.
2. **Pre-empt the 7-point demand** (PIR-PM-02) — Prepare point-by-point responses to Radzi's framework. The "full report" and "commit to MP proposals" demands are the most dangerous.
3. **Resolve the cross-era position** (PIR-PM-03) — The Aug 10 BN briefing is the decision point. The PM must decide before the floor forces a public position.
4. **Compile enforcement citables** (PIR-PM-06) — Have verified arrest/remand/investigation numbers ready for the floor. The Aug 9 remand expiry may produce new action.
5. **Manage backbenchers** (PIR-PM-07) — Identify and brief any PH backbencher considering exceeding the official line. Hassan Karim is the key watch.
6. **Stress-test the 75% claim** (PIR-PM-08) — Build a recommendation-by-recommendation matrix. Concede the unimplemented ones; defend the implemented ones with evidence.
