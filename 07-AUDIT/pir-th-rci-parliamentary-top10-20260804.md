## PIR Definitions

### PIR-TH-01: MP Accountability Demands — Who Named Whom?
**Original RCI PIR 1:** What individuals, office-holders and institutional bodies were responsible for the decisions identified by the RCI?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs have publicly demanded identification of the individuals responsible for the RCI-identified decisions? What specific names, offices, or periods have they named in statements, press conferences, or social media? Are they targeting specific individuals or systemic governance failures? Which MPs are defending named individuals?
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP statements naming individuals; demands for resignation; calls for prosecution; defence of named figures; distinction between collective vs individual responsibility; party-line vs dissident positions.

### PIR-TH-02: MP Enforcement Pressure — Demands for Consequences
**Original RCI PIR 2:** What is the present status of criminal, civil, regulatory and disciplinary action arising from the RCI findings?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are pressing for enforcement action (MACC, AGC, police, regulatory)? What specific cases or non-prosecution decisions are they challenging? Are any MPs demanding timelines, responsible agencies, or explanations for cases classified NFA? Which MPs are expressing satisfaction with enforcement pace?
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP demands for MACC/AGC action; challenges to NFA decisions; calls for forensic investigation; demands for prosecution timelines; ministerial responses to enforcement questions; PAC, PSC, or select committee involvement.

### PIR-TH-03: MP Fiscal Cost Scrutiny — Taxpayer Exposure Questions
**Original RCI PIR 3:** What was the complete financial cost of rescuing and restructuring Tabung Haji, and who ultimately bears that cost?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are questioning the taxpayer cost of the Tabung Haji rescue via Urusharta Jamaah? What specific figures, guarantees, or contingent liabilities are they demanding disclosure of? Are any MPs defending the rescue structure? What questions have been filed for the 11 August sitting on fiscal impact?
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP questions on Urusharta Jamaah cost; demands for government guarantee disclosure; sukuk obligation scrutiny; impairment and recovery value questions; fiscal exposure estimates cited by MPs.

### PIR-TH-04: MP Financial Resilience Assessment — Current Viability Questions
**Original RCI PIR 4:** How financially resilient is Tabung Haji today against renewed depositor withdrawals or deterioration in investment performance?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are questioning current Tabung Haji financial resilience? What independent verification are they demanding beyond self-reported figures? Are any MPs citing the RM95.1 billion deposits or 3.5% profit distribution figures? What questions are being raised about depositor protection?
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP questions on current liquidity; demands for stress-test results; depositor concentration concerns; reserve level inquiries; asset-liability mismatch questions; government guarantee exposure scrutiny.

### PIR-TH-05: MP Implementation Matrix Challenge — 25 Recommendations Audit
**Original RCI PIR 5:** Which of the RCI's 25 recommendations remain unimplemented, partially implemented or unverified?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are challenging the 75% implementation claim? Which specific recommendations are they flagging as unimplemented or partially implemented? Are any MPs presenting their own implementation matrices? What completion evidence are they demanding?
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP challenges to 75% claim; specific recommendation status queries; demands for independent validation; implementation deadline questions; responsible-owner identification requests.

### PIR-TH-06: MP Legislative Reform Proposals — Tabung Haji Act Amendments
**Original RCI PIR 6:** What legislative amendments are required to prevent future political or ministerial interference in Tabung Haji?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are proposing legislative amendments to the Tabung Haji Act 1995? What specific provisions are they targeting — ministerial powers, board appointments, politician eligibility, fiduciary duties, parliamentary oversight? Are any draft bills or private member's bills being discussed? What is the government's legislative timetable response?
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP proposals for Act amendments; specific clause references; ministerial power restriction proposals; board independence demands; politician eligibility restrictions; oversight mechanism proposals; government legislative timetable responses.

### PIR-TH-07: MP Hibah & Distribution Scrutiny — Political vs Financial
**Original RCI PIR 7:** Were profit distributions, hajj charges and financial assistance determined by financial capacity or political considerations?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are raising concerns about politically motivated hibah distributions? What transparency mechanisms are they proposing? Are any MPs defending past distributions as financially sound? What actuarial or accounting questions are they raising?
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP challenges to hibah methodology; demands for statutory distribution formula; political intervention allegations; accounting treatment questions; divergence between management advice and final decisions; beneficiary segmentation concerns.

### PIR-TH-08: MP Investment Accountability — 14 Troubled Investments
**Original RCI PIR 8:** What is the current position and recoverability of the troubled investments identified by the RCI?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are demanding forensic audits of the 14 investments? What transaction-level disclosure are they seeking? Are any MPs naming specific investments or beneficiaries? What recovery or disposal strategies are being proposed?
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP demands for forensic audit scope; specific investment naming; acquisition cost and current valuation queries; realised/unrealised loss figures cited; approval irregularity questions; legal limitation period concerns.

### PIR-TH-09: MP Hajj Affordability Position — Depositor Impact
**Original RCI PIR 9:** How will the RCI findings affect hajj affordability, financial assistance and waiting-list policy?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are connecting the RCI findings to hajj affordability concerns? What policy positions are they taking on subsidies, waiting periods, and deposit requirements? Are any MPs from Malay-Muslim majority constituencies raising depositor concerns? What demographic or Saudi cost factors are they citing?
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** MP statements on hajj cost impact; subsidy sustainability positions; waiting-list policy proposals; depositor constituency concerns; income-tier eligibility questions; Saudi operating cost changes cited; istito'ah vs affordability framing.

### PIR-TH-10: MP Narrative & Coalition Positioning — Political Risk Assessment
**Original RCI PIR 10:** What political, social and reputational narratives are emerging from the report, and how could they affect public confidence?
**Parliamentary Focus:** Which MPs are shaping public narratives around the RCI? How are party/coalition positions forming — PH, PN, BN, GPS, GRS, WARISAN, MUDA, Independent? Where are internal fissures emerging? Which MPs are escalating religious, racial, or institutional trust narratives? Which are attempting to de-escalate? What misinformation or distortion risks are MPs creating or amplifying?
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Open
**EEIs:** Party/coalition official positions; internal dissent signals; religious authority commentary by MP-aligned figures; social media narrative amplification by MPs; Malay-Muslim sentiment framing; depositor reaction exploitation; institutional trust attack/defence patterns; misinformation risk indicators.

2. **PAC Chairman & Members:** Public Accounts Committee — accountability focus
3. **Opposition Leader & Shadow Cabinet:** Primary critics — attack vectors
4. **PAS/Bersatu MPs:** Islamic credentials + Malay-Muslim constituency — religious/racial narrative risk

6. **Backbenchers (PH/PN):** Internal pressure points — dissidence signals
7. **East Malaysia MPs (GPS/GRS):** Coalition dynamics — leverage positions
8. **MPs from high-depositor constituencies:** Grassroots pressure — depositor sentiment proxy
