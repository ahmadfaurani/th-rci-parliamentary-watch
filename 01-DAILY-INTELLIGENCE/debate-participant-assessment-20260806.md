## 5. The Ismail Sabri Factor — How It Reshapes the Debate

**Timeline:**
- **Aug 7, 9am:** Ismail Sabri charged at KL Sessions Court (MACC Act, asset declaration)
- **Aug 10, 5pm:** BN pre-debate briefing (now overshadowed by Ismail Sabri charging)
- **Aug 11, 10am:** Special sitting

**Three scenarios:**

### Scenario A: Ismail Sabri attends the sitting (20% probability)
- He becomes the most-watched MP in the chamber
- Every PN MP will reference his charging as proof of BN corruption
- Zahid's "no one immune" is vindicated but BN's credibility collapses
- Ismail Sabri's Jul 29 "nothing to hide" statement becomes a viral liability
- He may attempt to speak in his own defence, creating a dramatic moment

### Scenario B: Ismail Sabri does NOT attend (60% probability)
- His absence is weaponised: "BN's former PM is so corrupt he can't even face Parliament"
- PN will demand he appear and explain
- Zahid must answer for Ismail Sabri — putting DPM in impossible position
- The RCI debate becomes a proxy for the Ismail Sabri criminal case

### Scenario C: Ismail Sabri attends but does not speak (20% probability)
- His physical presence is noted but he avoids the microphone
- PN MPs will point-mention him ("the MP for Bera is sitting right there")
- Maximum visual damage to BN with minimum verbal exposure

**Impact on other BN MPs:**
- Every BN MP will be asked "do you support Ismail Sabri?" before and during the debate
- Mohamad Hasan (Rembau) and Azalina (Pengerang) must distance or defend
- The Aug 10 BN briefing will now be consumed by Ismail Sabri's charging, not RCI strategy
