## Executive Summary
- **4th consecutive cycle with ZERO new sitting MP activity on the RCI.** No new RCI statements from any of the 222 sitting MPs. Total tracked remains 11. No position changes. The RCI story has fully faded from the Aug 6 evening news cycle — 5 days before the debate. All search queries (16 run) returned only existing Jul 29-Aug 5 MP statements or non-MP/civil-society content.
- **Umno AGM postponed to Sept 9-12 — AFTER the Aug 11 debate.** Announced by Umno Sec-Gen Asyraf Wajdi Dusuki (non-MP). This removes a potential pre-debate Umno RCI positioning venue and means Umno's internal grassroots RCI reckoning will happen post-debate. Umno MPs will enter the Aug 11 debate without having had their party AGM RCI discussion — the first Umno collective RCI reckoning now comes AFTER, not before, the parliamentary debate.

IDEAS' Pantau Kuasa research: 78% of chairperson positions across 135 federal statutory bodies have NO clearly defined qualification criteria. No MP has yet echoed this systemic dimension — it remains civil-society framing.
- **FMT adds RCI detail: 5 senior misconduct officers still employed at TH/subsidiaries as of 2022.** Penalties "watered down on appeal." Bonuses 2-13 months (2010-2017), 2014 special bonus RM74M. No MP has demanded accountability for these specific still-employed officers — a new accountability dimension that may surface in the Aug 11 debate.
- **BN Aug 10 briefing attendee list updated:** The Star (Aug 6) confirms SA Vigneswaran (MIC President, Senator, non-MP) among attendees alongside Mohamad Hasan (Rembau, MP), Wee Ka Siong (Ayer Hitam, MP). No new individual MP RCI positions from any attendee.
