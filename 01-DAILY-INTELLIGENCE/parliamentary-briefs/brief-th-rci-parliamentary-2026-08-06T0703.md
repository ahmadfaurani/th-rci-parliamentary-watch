## Intelligence Gaps

1. **Identity of 35-40 MPs ready to debate** — Fahmi announced the number but no MP names have been released. We don't know which parties, coalitions, or constituencies they represent. This is critical pre-debate intelligence.
2. **BN's collective position** — The Aug 10 briefing will determine BN's stance, but we don't know if they'll accept historical accountability, push cross-era deflection, or coordinate with PN. Outcome won't be known until Aug 10.
3. **Speaker Johari Abdul's procedural rules** — Will the Speaker allow naming of individuals? Time limits per MP? Will questions be permitted or speeches only? No information on debate format beyond "continuous, no break."
4. **Which minister responds** — Zulkifli (Senator) is presenting, but who answers MP questions? Anwar as PM? Finance Minister? Creates structural gap if a Senator presents but MPs question.
5. **MACC Aug 9 remand outcome** — Whether the 3 individuals are released or charged will shape the enforcement narrative heading into the debate.
6. **PAC position** — Mas Ermieyati (PAC Chair) has not signalled any PAC action on the RCI. The PAC has fiscal oversight authority but hasn't used it.
7. **PKR Congress RCI agenda** — Whether the RCI debate outcome will be addressed at the Aug 15-16 congress is unknown. Current agenda shows no RCI item.
8. **Fuziah Salleh's role** — As PKR Sec-Gen and former Deputy Minister for Religious Affairs (2018-2020), her Jul 30 statement is the most detailed PKR position. But as a Senator, she can't speak at the Dewan Rakyat debate. Does this create a gap in PKR's parliamentary voice on RCI?
