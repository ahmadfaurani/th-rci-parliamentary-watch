The framing split could sharpen at the Aug 11 debate. Watch for MPs choosing sides between criminal-prosecution and structural-reform framings.
- **Recovery-narrative challenge — AMBER.** Non-MP Rafizi's claim that TH "is still exposed to sustainability risk" directly contests the government's "75% implemented, TH restored" line. If any sitting MP (PH backbencher or opposition) echoes this, it becomes a RED narrative escalation. Currently confined to non-MP commentary.
- **Intra-BN "sakau" pressure — AMBER.** UMNO Youth's question "which UMNO politician sakau?" (via non-MP Akmal) creates internal BN tension, pressuring named figures (e.g., Azeez). No sitting MP has echoed this yet, but it is a live fissure.

- **Misinformation risk — GREEN/AMBER.** The "sakau" word does not appear in the RCI report (per prior-cycle Malaysiakini analysis) yet drives political framing — continued risk of narrative distortion by MPs at the debate.
