## Executive Summary
- **MACC makes first concrete enforcement arrests (Aug 3-5): 4 individuals detained** — ex-CEO (68) and ex-CFO (60) over RM370M plantation share deal; company director over RM300K renovation bribery; former estate manager over RM8M contract misappropriation. All linked to TH RCI. **CRITICAL: NO sitting MP has reacted to any arrest** — a notable silence given that multiple MPs previously demanded enforcement (PIR-TH-02).
- **Tracker corrections: Azeez is NOT a sitting MP.** The Star explicitly calls him "former Baling MP." Current Baling MP is Bahador Shah Md. Isa (UMNO, GE15 2022). Previous cycle INCORRECTLY tracked Azeez as a sitting MP. Additionally, Mujahid Yusof Rawa (who claimed RCI vindication) is a Senator since May 2023, NOT a sitting MP — his statements are out of scope. Muhammad Faiz Fadzil (Amanah Sec-Gen who demanded MACC probes) also lost Tampin in GE15 — not a sitting MP.
- **RCI findings reveal political interference at granular level:** Malaysiakini analysis (Jul 29) confirms RCI named Abdul Azeez (then MP & UMNO supreme council → TH Chairman, held 8 concurrent subsidiary positions), Badruddin Amiruldin (former MP → board member), and Rosni Sohar (ADUN → board member) as political figures on the TH board. RCI found hibah, haj charges, and HAFIS decisions were politically driven. Post-2018, Azmin Ali's "exco perniagaan" replaced the investment panel without legal basis — governance gap.
- **RCI witness testimony EXCLUDED from public report** — Malaysiakini confirmed testimony from 9 key witnesses (Azeez, Ismee, Mujahid, Jamil Khir, etc.) was NOT quoted or summarized. This directly supports PIR-TH-E03 (report completeness) and Ahmad Fadhli's "what else is hidden?" narrative.
- **RCI recommends specific TH Act 1995 (Act 535) amendments** — bar politicians from boards, restructure ministerial powers (separate haj from finance), formalize Investment Panel/Syariah Advisory/Haj Management committees. No sitting MP has yet proposed specific legislative text, but the RCI has laid the groundwork for PIR-TH-06.

## Narrative Alert Assessment
- **"Justice delayed" / "what else is hidden" framing — AMBER.** Ahmad Fadhli (PN/PAS) escalates with "justice delayed is justice denied" and questions whether other documents are being hidden. This directly supports the report-completeness narrative (PIR-TH-E03) and could sharpen at the Aug 11 debate. If more MPs echo this, it becomes RED.
- **Cross-era accountability framing — AMBER.** Zahid's cabinet expansion to include PH/PN eras introduces a new narrative dimension: accountability is no longer just about 2014-2018 (BN era) but extends to 2018-2020 (PH/PN era). The RCI found post-2018 governance gaps (Azmin's exco perniagaan). This framing battle will shape the Aug 11 debate. Watch for MPs choosing sides.
- **Enforcement-validation vs. enforcement-critique — GREEN/AMBER.** MACC has made 4 arrests. If MPs frame this as "the system is working," it de-escalates. If MPs frame it as "too little, too late" or "selective prosecution," it escalates. Currently, no MP has framed it either way — the silence itself is notable.
- **"Sakau" vs. governance-failure framing — AMBER.** PM's "sakau/treachery" (criminal) vs. Syed Saddiq's "governance not embezzlement" (structural) split persists. Amanah's party statement adds a third dimension: "unity of ummah" agenda critique.
- **Religious/institutional-trust framing — GREEN.** No MP has escalated religious or racial framing. Engagement remains fiscal/governance-focused.
- **Misinformation risk — GREEN/AMBER.** Page count discrepancy (150/204/211) and "sakau" term not appearing in RCI report — continued risk of narrative distortion.
