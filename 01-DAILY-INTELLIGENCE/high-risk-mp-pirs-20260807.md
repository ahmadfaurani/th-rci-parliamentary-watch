The PM or Fahmi may frame the charging as proof that "no one is immune" and "the system is working." This is risky because: (a) if the charges are NOT RCI-related, the connection is false and can be rebutted; (b) it may anger BN/UMNO MPs who see a coalition partner's former leader being weaponised; (c) it blurs the line between RCI accountability and general corruption.
