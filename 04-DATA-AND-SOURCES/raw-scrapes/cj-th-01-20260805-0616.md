## Finding 2 — NEW IN-SCOPE MP (POSITION EVOLUTION)
**Source:** Sinar Harian
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**URL:** https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/790728/berita/semasa/radzi-gesa-audit-forensik-pelaburan-rm259-juta-th
**MP Named:** Dr Mohd Radzi Jidin (Putrajaya MP, Bersatu VP, PN)
**Summary:** Called for forensic audit of TH's RM259 million investment in Deru Semangat Sdn Bhd, citing the RCI report. Disclosed full transaction details: RM526.16M total approved (2014-2015); RM231M for 55% share purchase; RM295.16M for plantation development; ~RM257M paid out; final RM259M disbursed (RCI p.143). Project involved logging in a forest reserve in Pahang. Investment value crashed from RM257M to RM32M; TH's shares were returned to the original owner and TH no longer holds any shares. Radzi questioned: was due diligence done? Was forest-reserve land status checked? What benefit did TH/depositors get from RM259M? Noted RM259M exceeds TH's 2026 hajj financial assistance of RM210M. The investment was approved by the Minister in the PM's Dept (Islamic Affairs) — implicating political oversight.
**PIR Mapping:** PIR-TH-01, PIR-TH-08
**Confidence:** HIGH — direct on-record, detailed transaction figures with RCI page reference
**Tag:** forensic-audit-demand, accountability-demand, transaction-level-disclosure, position-evolution
**NOTE:** POSITION EVOLUTION — Radzi escalated from procedural demands (per-recommendation status, debate time) to naming a SPECIFIC investment with transaction-level disclosure. Most granular transaction-level demand by any MP to date. Links approval to the Islamic Affairs minister.
