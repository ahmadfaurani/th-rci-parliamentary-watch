## Finding 4: RCI Recommends Amending TH Act 1995 (Act 535) — Specific Provisions Identified

**Source:** Malay Mail
**Date:** 30 Jul 2026
**URL:** https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/30/tabung-haji-rci-report-reveals-political-interference-in-pilgrim-funds-management/229486
**MP Named:** None (RCI recommendations, not MP statements)
**Summary:** RCI found TH Act 1995 (Act 535) outdated, confers overly broad ministerial powers. Minister used power to terminate CEO Nik Mohd Hasyudeen Yusoff (May 5, 2021, before term expiry Aug 31, 2021) and Chairman Tan Sri Md Nor Md Yusof (Oct 15, 2021, before renewed term expiry). RCI recommends: (1) Amend Act 535 to strengthen governance/regulatory framework; (2) Restructure roles — haj management under Minister in PM Dept (Religious Affairs), financial/fund/investment management under Finance Minister; (3) Formalize Investment Panel, Syariah Advisory Committee, Haj Management Committee under Act 535.
**PIR Mapping:** PIR-TH-06 (legislative reform), PIR-TH-07 (hibah/transparency)
**Confidence:** HIGH — Malay Mail analysis of RCI report
**Tag:** legislative-reform, governance-overhaul
